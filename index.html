02 — Grynox · Founder · 2026

One shoot.
Many hooks.

I founded and launched Grynox — maker of the X-POD phone mount — and ran its marketing end to end. I built ads as testing systems: the same footage — shot for real or 100% AI-generated — cut into different hooks, angles and styles, to let the data pick the winner. The result: strong product sales.

Test 1 — Hook angles

Same creator, same footage — three different openings.

Only the first seconds change. Everything after the hook stays constant, so the test isolates what actually grabs attention.

1Shoot once
2Cut 3 hooks
3Launch & read the data
Test 2 — Creative styles

Same materials — four completely different ads.

Creator-led, infographic, benefit-led ("more views") and how-to ("shoot with your phone"). Different styles reach different people; testing them side by side shows which one scales.

2Four creative concepts
3Scale the winner
